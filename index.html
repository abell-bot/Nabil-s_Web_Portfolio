PROJECT 02

Real Men Reads Labels First

Basic Health Toxicology

10 June 2026

A Health Promotion Day booth for Basic Health Toxicology (MDU1163, Group 3) that encourages men to read personal care labels before they buy.

Real Men Reads Labels First was our group's Health Promotion Day project for MDU1163 Basic Health Toxicology (Group 3), held at BHEP UNIMAS on 10 June 2026 under the theme Risk Assessment to Personal Care. The booth asked visitors to check what is in their skincare, colognes and hair gels before using them.

The poster and booth content were built around four simple habits:

  • Ingredients check: look out for harmful ingredients such as mercury and parabens before buying.
  • Research before buying: make sure products are NPRA-approved and suitable for your skin type.
  • Storage tips: keep products in a clean, dry place at room temperature, not in hot and damp bathrooms.
  • PAO symbol: read the Period After Opening jar symbol (6M, 12M or 24M) to know how long a product stays safe once opened.

To keep visitors interested, the booth ran a Kahoot! quiz challenge and a hands-on Smell and Guess challenge with freebies. A QR code linked to the official Malaysian NPRA website so people could look up their products on the spot.

EduSmart App System

Java · SDG 4

5 June 2026

A simulated learning app built with Java Swing and aligned with SDG 4 (Quality Education), bringing study content and quizzes into one desktop window.

EduSmart is a simulated education app written in Java with a Swing graphical interface (QuizGUI). It is designed around SDG 4, Quality Education, and brings study material and quizzes together in a single EDUSMART LEARNING HUB window, so a learner can read the lecture content and then test what they have learned.

PROJECT 04

DirectFarm Samarahan

Object Oriented Software Development · Python

30 December 2025

A Python system that cuts out the middleman by connecting farmers directly with customers, with a separate menu for each of four user roles.

DirectFarm Samarahan is a menu-driven Python system I built for my Object Oriented Software Development course. It follows the whole journey of a farm sale: a farmer lists produce, a buyer places an order, a village coordinator arranges the delivery, and a driver completes the trip.

The program opens with a main menu for the four roles: Farmer, Buyer, Coordinator and Driver. Behind it, the data is organised into produce, order, payment, delivery trip and settlement records. Each record gets an automatically generated code such as ORD001, so every transaction can be traced. The system also handles customer calculations and fare routing for village coordinators.

Highlights

  • Python: console menus, lists that store the records, and a helper function that creates sequential, zero-padded codes from a prefix.
  • Object-oriented design: the system is split into classes for produce, orders, payments, delivery trips and settlements.
  • Role-based flow: each user role only sees the actions that belong to it.
PROJECT 05

Anatomy Drawing and Details

Drawing & Art

12 November 2025

A graphite pencil study of the human figure, drawn to build my anatomy and shading skills for an elective course.

For my elective course I practised drawing the human body, studying how posture, proportion and weight move through the figure. This piece is a hand-drawn graphite study of a figure arching backwards, signed in the bottom corner.

I focused on light and shadow: soft hatching in the background, darker and denser strokes for the swimsuit, and a cast shadow near the feet to ground the figure on the page.

Highlights

  • Shading: building tone with graphite, from light hatching to deep shadow.
  • Practice: steady drawing practice to upgrade my skills across the course.
